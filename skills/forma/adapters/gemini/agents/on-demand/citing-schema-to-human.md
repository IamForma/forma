# Citing the schema to the human — translate on the spot

Read this file when you're about to quote or paraphrase schema text (`.claude/rules/claude-8.md`/`.agents/rules/gemini-8.md`/`.codex/CODEX-8.md`, role files) to the human in conversation — `Intent`/`Kit`/`Core` only, the three nodes with an outward voice (`AGENTS.md` §1, "Five pairs"); `Spec`/`Run` never speak to the human and never need this file.

**The schema files themselves (`.claude/rules/claude-8.md`/`.agents/rules/gemini-8.md`/`.codex/CODEX-8.md`, role files) stay in English — the token saving is real (roughly two-fifths) and this rule doesn't reopen it.** Quoting or paraphrasing their text to the human in conversation, when the conversation's language differs from English (the project language, `project/config/PROJECT.md`) — translate on the spot; a raw English quote handed to the human, left for them to work out themselves, doesn't satisfy this.
