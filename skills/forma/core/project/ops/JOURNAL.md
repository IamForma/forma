# JOURNAL — cycle journal

What changed in the structure and in experience, cycle by cycle. New entries go on top.

**Filled in by `Intent` when a cycle closes.** Not a separate ritual: it is what its decision about the next cycle is made of.

**Rule:** entries are not rewritten after the fact. A cycle that failed stays in the journal as it is — a year on it is more useful than a successful one, because it shows on which decision things diverged.

## How to read the numbers

The values say nothing by themselves — what matters is the direction over three or four cycles. No number is read alone: spend that fell together with the share passing the check is not savings but an unfinished cycle.

**The share passing the check first time grows** — slicing is getting better. Standing still with a growing number of tasks — also good. Falling — slicing cannot keep up with the growing complexity, time to bring back the trial run or raise the tier on the specification.

**Returns at the form check fall** — the specification has learned to write tasks out in full. Holding at one level — something is systematically missing: look at which of the five items is missing most often, that is the blind spot.

**Attempts over budget fall** — planning is becoming realistic. Growing — either the tasks are getting more complex, or budgets are set from what is wanted, not from past cycles.

**`Core` escalations fall** — the structure has settled. Not falling — the consequence is being fixed, not the cause: look at which node `Core` escalates to most often, the defect is there.

**Cycle spend falls with the same number of tasks** — the tooling pays off: slicing is more precise, fewer attempts. Growing faster than the number of tasks — the work is pulled by the wrong tier: look where full effort is spent on checking against a written criterion.

**All hold still for several cycles in a row** — the system works evenly and does not improve. This is the quietest failure: everything is in order, each cycle resembles the last. Check whether the "What was missing" field is being filled in — most likely it is empty.
